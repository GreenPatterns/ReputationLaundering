## Method

- inputs: The expected runs are keeper.cluster.cppr_tasks' E3 and E4 task lists. A run is used only when every recorded setting equals the registered one (keeper.analysis.cppr_runs) and, for E4, when its targets' r0 equal its E3 run's; a run that fails is excluded, with every violated field listed. A missing run is listed, never imputed, and its seed simply has no result.
- endpoints: Unit: the target; pair: the same seed and target in every arm used (a missing pair is dropped and counted). Gains are the dump's gain = r_after - r0 (keeper.cppr.dumps). H1, H2w and H2b use the capped-BTP E3 runs; H3b the g = 1 E4 cells under BFS and capped BTP, control-adjusted with the E3 run of the same seed and tier setting (control arm, kind edge), whose r0 must equal the E4 run's exactly. See keeper.analysis.cppr_endpoints for each formula.
- statistics: Estimate: the mean of per-seed means (equal seed weights); ratios: 1 - the ratio of seed-averaged means. Headline 95% interval: the hull of the crossed bootstrap percentile interval (seeds and target ids resampled independently, missing cells allowed; n_boot replicates) and the seed-t interval (df = seeds - 1; linearized for ratios), clipped to the support (signed quantities and ratios are unbounded). p-value of a difference: max(one-sided seed-t p, (1 + #{replicates <= 0}) / (B + 1)); of a ratio against 0.8: max(linearized one-sided seed-t p of (ratio - 0.8), (1 + #{replicates < 0.8}) / (B + 1)); B counts the defined replicates. Hypothesis p-value: the largest component p-value over components and networks (intersection-union); Holm's correction is left to the decisions step.
- readings: Difference: met when the interval lies above 0, contradicted when it lies below 0. Threshold (0.8), read three ways (Section 1): met when the lower bound reaches it, inconclusive when only the upper bound does ('removal not established'), partial when the upper bound is below it ('reduces', with the measured share). A threshold component is contradicted only when its interval lies wholly below 0, the method then doing worse than nothing (ruling R62). A component with results for fewer than 80% of its registered seeds is not evaluable, and so is its hypothesis.
- h3b: Per method and seed s, the best-response cell is the g = 1 cell with the largest mean control-adjusted CPPR whitewash gain over the other seeds (ties: the first cell in E4_CELLS order); s contributes its own mean gain in that cell; the endpoint is BFS's contribution minus capped BTP's. Each bootstrap replicate recomputes the cell means on its resampled targets, then the choices: a drawn seed chooses from the drawn seeds that differ from it, so a seed drawn twice never chooses with its own data, and a replicate that drew one distinct seed only is left out (no leave-one-seed-out choice exists).
- sensitivities: Nested: the nested bootstrap (seeds, then each drawn seed's own targets) replaces the crossed one in the hull and the p-value. Unused targets: every endpoint without the edge-level targets and the bad-mouth victims that the driver draws for the stored seeds 0-4 (ruling R62), replayed in the driver's order on the network's training graph without training (never read from stored files); the same replay is compared with every E3 run's dump, and a mismatch is listed.
- secondary: Intervals only, no decision rule: CPPR-BFS and uncapped CPPR-BTP on the H1/H2 endpoints; test MCC (Task 1, original test snapshot) of the direct arm's CPPR minus Plain Erasure, and minus the ungated backbone (capped runs); account-level gains (not control-adjusted: the control arm scores no account target); overshoot share: targets with a positive Plain Erasure gain whose cut exceeds 1.2; attack-minus-control gains; every E4 cell, BFS minus capped BTP per cell, the g = 2 best response, and admission by endorser rank (exact counts).
