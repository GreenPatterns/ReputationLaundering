## Notes for the paper

Every item below is computed from the values when this summary is written.

- Legacy endorsed set D (keeper.credibility.tiers.legacy_bfs). D covers more than half of the active accounts on Bitcoin-Alpha (95.2%) and Bitcoin-OTC (91.3%), and fewer than half on Wiki-RfA (22.8%). During its single pass, one positive rating from a member of D admits the recipient, and a ring of accounts that rate each other positively after that rating enters D as well. Share of the accounts first active after snapshot 4 that are in D: 93.1% on Bitcoin-Alpha, 88.2% on Bitcoin-OTC and 19.5% on Wiki-RfA. Tier 0 share of the active accounts: 7.2% on Bitcoin-Alpha, 12.9% on Bitcoin-OTC and 84.3% on Wiki-RfA.
- Single warning. The engine keeps witness evidence live for H = 6 snapshots and closes a record at A = 13.28. Under these rules, a warning issued one snapshot after a rating revokes 64.7% of it at the ceiling hazard lambda = 2, the hazard of Fig. 1c and the lag census. One fully reliable witness gives lambda^1 = 0.533, and lambda = 0.53 revokes 46.8%. One witness at the floor 0.20 gives lambda^1 = 0.102, and lambda = 0.10 revokes 16.4%. Eq. 8 has no expiry and gives 64.7%, 47.0% and 17.4%, so the expiry matters only for a weak hazard. The engine mixes a rating's two channels by its routing. One warning at lag 1 therefore revokes 44.0% of a negative and 46.2% of a positive rating from a fully reliable witness, and 14.5% and 16.1% at the floor. Without the expiry these become 44.3% and 46.4%, and 15.4% and 17.1%. Reaching lambda^1 >= 1.9 takes 7 fully reliable witnesses.
- CPPR seed-level statistics, pre-revision runs (superseded by E3). The paired Plain VPE - CPPR whitewash gain, with its 95% headline interval (ruling R30) and paired t statistic, is Bitcoin-Alpha: +0.055 [+0.004, +0.106], t = 3.00 (df 4), p = 0.0397; Bitcoin-OTC: +0.048 [+0.002, +0.094], t = 2.87 (df 4), p = 0.0453; Wiki-RfA: +0.322 [+0.252, +0.393], t = 12.69 (df 4), p = 0.0002. The interval of the paired gain excludes 0 on Bitcoin-Alpha, Bitcoin-OTC and Wiki-RfA. The Plain VPE whitewash gain, the reference of the attack cut, has an interval that includes 0 on Bitcoin-Alpha (+0.055 [-0.004, +0.113]). The CPPR whitewash gain has an interval above 0 on Bitcoin-OTC (+0.024 [+0.006, +0.042]). The per-seed CPPR - Plain Erasure test MCC takes both signs on Bitcoin-Alpha (-0.053 to +0.070), Bitcoin-OTC (-0.077 to +0.074) and Wiki-RfA (-0.066 to +0.010).
